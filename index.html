What is PHASE?

A structured system for movement, breath & somatic awareness.

The PHASE Method™ is a structured movement system developed by Elise El Khoury, PT, combining functional mobility, breathwork, and somatic awareness within a holistic framework.

Designed for both personal and professional development, the method provides practical tools to improve movement, reduce physical tension, and support nervous-system regulation.

About Elise

Elise El Khoury

Physiotherapist · Founder of the PHASE Method™

Our Philosophy

Your body isn't working against you — it's guiding you.

PHASE is more than a studio. It's a space to come home to yourself. Through intentional movement, conscious breath, and somatic awareness, we help you release tension, rebuild strength, and move through every phase of your life with calm and clarity.

Calm is your superpower. Show up for yourself.

In the Studio

Find your phase

Four ways to move, each tuned to where you are today. Reformer-based and mat practice, guided with intention.

01

Restore · Mobility · Breath

Reset Phase

A slower, restorative practice. Release built-up tension, open the body, and down-regulate the nervous system through guided breath and gentle mobility. The reset your week is asking for.

02

Strength · Stability · Control

Core Phase

Your core is your powerhouse. Protect it, train it, and it will carry you. A focused, strengthening session building deep stability, control, and full-body coordination.

03

Mat · Flow · Alignment

Mat Pilates

Classical Pilates on the mat — precise, flowing, and grounding. Build lean strength and body awareness using your own resistance, breath, and intentional alignment.

04

Signature · Complete · Balanced

Reset + Core

The signature PHASE experience. The restorative release of Reset woven together with the strength of Core — a complete, balanced session that leaves you steady, open, and strong.

The Founder

Elise El Khoury

Physiotherapist · Founder of PHASE Method™

Elise is a physiotherapist with advanced academic and clinical training in pediatric rehabilitation, respiratory therapy, and psychosomatic approaches. She created the PHASE Method™ to bridge the gap between evidence-based rehabilitation and holistic movement.

Her work is built on a simple belief: movement is a conversation with the body, not a battle against it. At PHASE, every session blends clinical insight with breath, mobility, and genuine care — so you leave feeling stronger, calmer, and more at home in yourself.
